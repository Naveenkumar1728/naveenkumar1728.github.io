Demos coming December 2026

A no-LLM artificial-life network of threads and junction points where vibrations travel and points fire, with a short list of hand-designed rules.

Question
Can it keep itself in a lively-but-stable state on its own?

Nyrel

Demos coming December 2026

A voice AI whose emotions and memories are earned through conversation, not scripted.

  • Speech in/out
  • Interruptible real-time listening
  • Emotion steering
  • Long-term memory
  • Safety layer
Result
The model’s pretraining overrode the origin I taught her. In a pre-registered test, telling her five times beat it: 7 of 10 simulated lives, vs 0 of 10 when told once.
Follow-up
A 125-hour pre-registered follow-up failed: 5 of 10 vs the 7 needed. It showed why: her memory ranks moments by emotional weight, and being told her origin scored no higher than small talk. Controls held (0 of 10). Next test: December.
Now
In progress A long-life test (about 79 hours on my laptop) checks what she keeps when memory space runs out.

Physics

Radiative Energy Balance Framework (REBF)

v1 preprint, 2026 Not peer-reviewed

A framework paper in gravitational-wave physics, not a new theory. It shows that the energy a black-hole merger loses can be counted three equivalent ways that must agree, the “energy triangle”, and states when this applies and when it doesn’t.

  • 81events from the public GWTC-4.0 catalog
  • 1077–5×1078gravitons per event
  • 3equivalent counts that must agree
  • Applied to real LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA data: graviton counts for 81 events in the public GWTC-4.0 catalog (1077 to 5×1078 each), confirming every detected signal is deeply classical.
  • Honest negative result: the proposed catalog check turned out to be circular on current public data, and the paper says so.
  • Fully reproducible: code and data included.
  • Origin: it grew out of my first paper, the Theory of Singular Displacement.
